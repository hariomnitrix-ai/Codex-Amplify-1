# Compliance gate

Every target record must identify permission basis, terms URL, robots review where applicable, review date, allowed hosts, data fields, personal-data status, retention, and rate limits. A human review is mandatory for ambiguity, authentication, user-generated content, sensitive/personal data, anti-bot barriers, paywalls, unclear licenses, or source objections.

The factory must stop—never escalate evasion—on CAPTCHA, access denial, robots/terms changes, or cease-and-desist signals. It may reduce rate, pause, quarantine, and request approval. Publication approval does not override law or target terms.
